Delete your data — Stack Hop: Tower Blocks

From Circumpolar Apps, developer of Stack Hop: Tower Blocks on Google Play (com.circumpolarapps.stackhop). Updated 15-October-2026.

The game has no accounts and no servers of ours: we do not hold your game data. Your progress is on your phone and, if you use Google Play Games, in your Google account. The steps below delete it. You do not need to contact us, but you can.

1. Delete your game progress (in the game)

  1. Open Stack Hop.
  2. Tap the gear (Settings) on the home screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Reset Progress, then Reset.

This deletes your coins, characters and themes earned in play, best scores, records, missions, achievements, powerups and streaks on the phone. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the saved copy of your progress in your Google account is replaced with the empty one at the same time.

To remove everything from the phone, including your settings, uninstall the game, or go to Android's Settings → Apps → Stack Hop → Storage → Clear data.

2. Delete your Google Play Games data (saved game and leaderboard scores)

On your phone:

  1. Open the Google Play Games app.
  2. At the top of the screen, tap More, then Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Play Games account & data.
  4. Under Delete individual game data, find Stack Hop and tap Delete.

Or on the web: go to play.google.com/games/profile, click Your Data, then Delete Play Games Account & Data, and delete Stack Hop under Delete individual game data. Google's own help page: Delete your Play Games data or Play Games profile.

This removes the game's saved copy and your scores on its leaderboards. Google carries it out under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

3. Game statistics and ads

Google Analytics for Firebase and Google AdMob record how the game is used and which ads are shown, under an app instance ID and your device's advertising ID, not your name. We cannot link them to you.

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device).
  • Uninstalling the game ends its app instance ID; statistics already recorded are deleted automatically by Google Analytics after at most 14 months.
  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you can withdraw consent at any time in the game's Settings → Ad Privacy, which stops statistics at once.
