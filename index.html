Privacy Policy — Stack Hop: Tower Blocks

Effective date: 15-September-2026

This privacy policy explains how Circumpolar Apps ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile game Stack Hop: Tower Blocks (the "App", package com.circumpolarapps.stackhop) on Android.

Summary

  • The App has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or other contact details.
  • Your game progress stays on your device.
  • The App shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device information (explained below).
  • Payments for in-app purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

1. Information stored on your device

The App saves your game progress on your device: scores, coins, unlocked characters and themes, powerups, mission and achievement progress, tutorial progress, purchase status and settings such as volume. We do not send this data to any server, and we cannot access it. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes it for good. The App does not back this data up to the cloud.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads through Google AdMob, an advertising service from Google LLC. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (such as your country)
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, app version and network type
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown or tapped
  • diagnostic data, such as crash logs and performance data related to ads

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. Read more about how Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland). If you are in one of these regions, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent message before it shows personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad Privacy in the App.

Your controls. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device). Buying "Remove Ads" in the App turns off the forced ads. Optional rewarded ads, which you choose to watch for a bonus, may still be offered.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your name, email address or payment card details. The App receives only what it needs to deliver your purchase, such as the product bought and a purchase confirmation from Google Play. Google's privacy policy covers Google Play: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Permissions

The App uses the Internet and network state permissions to load ads and to process purchases. The App's ad SDK uses the advertising ID permission (AD_ID). The App does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

5. How we use information

We do not collect personal information ourselves. The third-party services above process information only to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and process purchases. We do not sell your personal information.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the App are limited to content suitable for general audiences. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Data security and retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete it. Google holds the data it collects for ads and purchases for as long as its own policies state.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA/UK or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to or limit how it is processed. We do not keep personal information linked to you, so most requests are best sent to Google through the links above. You can also contact us and we will help where we can.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when we add new features. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at: circumpolarapps@gmail.com
